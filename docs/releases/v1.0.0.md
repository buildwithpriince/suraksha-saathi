Suraksha Saathi v1.0.0 is the SIH 2026 (PS 26041) submission build: camera-based safety drills for new industrial workers in Jharkhand, action-based scoring, and QR certificates that can be issued and verified with no network.

## Install the APK (outside the Play Store)

Android 10 or newer. No headset and no ARCore needed.

1. On the phone, download `suraksha-saathi-v1.0.0.apk` from the Assets below.
2. Open it. When Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps** (Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps).
3. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect says the app is unrecognised, choose **More details → Install anyway**.
4. Open Suraksha Saathi, set the site code (for example `DHN-01`) and enrol a worker.
5. Optional: print the exit and hazard markers from [`mobile/assets/markers/`](https://github.com/buildwithpriince/suraksha-saathi/tree/v1.0.0/mobile/assets/markers) for the evacuation step and marker lock.

Everything the worker does works in airplane mode.

## What's in it

- **FIRE_01 Fire & explosion** and **GAS_01 Gas leak & confined space**, drawn over the live camera on the real floor, or in a drawn virtual room when there is no camera.
- **PASS extinguisher technique**: drag the pin out, aim at the base, squeeze the lever, sweep, with a fire that responds to what you do.
- **Action-based scoring**: 9 rules per module, 70% to pass, critical steps that fail the attempt whatever the score, and a result screen that explains every lost point out loud.
- **Offline certificates**: signed QR issued on the phone; two-level Ed25519 chain (root → device attestation → certificate); offline verification in the app and on the web verify page, with a cached revocation list.
- **Refresher drills** of the critical steps on day 7 and day 30 after the first pass, and a retention chart on the dashboard.
- **Wallet cards**: worker ID card (its QR opens the worker's training on a shared phone) and certificate card, shareable as an image; printable ID card sheet.
- **English, Hindi and Santali (Ol Chiki)**, switchable at runtime, with spoken instructions.
- **Compliance dashboard**: sites, workers, attempts, certificates, compliance heatmap, recertification due, revocation, CSV export, retention chart.

## Known limitations

- **Certificates are signed with the published TEST keys.** The app shows a "Demo keys" banner; they are not valid outside the demo. The real root key ceremony is a deployment step (SETUP.md section 8).
- **The phone does not sync to the backend yet.** The backend sync API is built and tested; the app's sync client is on the roadmap. The dashboard demo runs on seeded data.
- **Santali text is a machine draft under native-speaker review.** The app says so on the Home screen. Android has no Santali voice, so Santali narration is spoken with the Hindi text-to-speech voice while the Santali caption stays on screen.
- **Narration is text-to-speech**, not recorded audio, in every language.
- **Overlays hold their direction, not their position.** Without ARCore world tracking they drift if you walk, except while the printed hazard marker is in view; "Reposition" re-places them.
- **Kiosk mode is partial.** Scanning a worker ID card opens that worker's page; there is no locked worker session yet.
- Only two of the problem statement's safety domains are built; the others are content work on the roadmap.
